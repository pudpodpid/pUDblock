pUDblock helps filter ads while you watch Twitch live streams.

When an ad break is detected, pUDblock looks for a compatible clean stream at your current video quality. If one is available, playback can continue through the break. If no clean stream is available, it hides and mutes the player with a waiting screen until clean video returns. Ad traffic may still download during this waiting period.

The popup lets you switch filtering and alternate streams on or off, check activity for the current tab, and see how ads are being handled.

Playlist processing happens locally. pUDblock does not send viewing history, analytics, or logs to its developer. Playback requests go directly to Twitch and its streaming servers.

Free and open source. No pUDblock account, subscription, or payment is required.

Designed for Twitch live streams. Clips, past broadcasts, and other websites are not supported. Twitch changes and stream availability can affect results; uninterrupted or completely ad-free playback is not guaranteed. The hidden-ad fallback does not support picture-in-picture or casting.

pUDblock is an independent project and is not affiliated with Twitch.
